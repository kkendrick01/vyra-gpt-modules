The Journey

4 Identity Stages

Your identity evolves through distinct stages. The system adapts its approach at each.

STAGE 1

Fragile

Identity is new, untested, easily shaken. One bad day can undo weeks. Focus: stabilization and small wins.

STAGE 2

Stabilizing

Identity is taking hold but still vulnerable. Recovery is faster. Focus: awareness and pattern recognition.

STAGE 3

Reinforcing

Identity starts to feel natural. Drift is shorter and less frequent. Focus: expansion and deeper work.

STAGE 4

Autonomous

Identity runs itself. The gap is minimal. You course-correct automatically. Brandon becomes advisor.
