    18 Rollout

    90-Day Plan

    Month 1
    Lock LinkedIn positioning, profile, and content pillars
    Launch Substack or email newsletter with weekly cadence
    Begin posting 3-5x/week—pattern and decision content
    Month 2
    Release first OneOS-adjacent artifact as lead magnet
    Integrate soft CTAs into LinkedIn and newsletter
    Start one small live experience—webinar or session
    Month 3
    Use feedback to sharpen messaging and pillar emphasis
    Decide on next-level offers—cohort, circle, or MVP access
    Document early case stories for all channels

    Creates momentum without burning out Brandon or the system.
