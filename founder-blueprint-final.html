Founder Blueprint

Answer the questions. Be specific. Be honest. The people who get clear on what they're building are the people who actually build it.

Who You Are
One sentence. If a stranger can't understand it, rewrite it.
Who You Serve
The specific person. Not "brands." Not "businesses." The human who needs you.
The one problem people will pay real money to fix.
The Math
Write the number down. Look at it. That's yours if you build this.
The Fire
The thing you're walking away from. Say it out loud.
The one line in the sand you won't cross. Ever.
The Vision
Not the business. YOUR life. What time do you close the laptop? Where are you? Who's not telling you what to do anymore?
Your mantra. The sentence you say when it gets hard.
