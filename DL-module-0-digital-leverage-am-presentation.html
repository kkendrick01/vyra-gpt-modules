CLIENTS

The Foundation of Trust

The individuals and organizations under your care. Their goals, expectations, and experience define the success of your role.

Primary Responsibility: Build and maintain trust by delivering clarity, predictability, and consistent results.

CORE RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Set clear expectations during onboarding
  • Understand each client's goals, worldview, tone, and constraints
  • Build a predictable weekly communication rhythm
  • Track progress toward goals and note blockers
  • Handle escalations with calm and accuracy
  • Maintain retention through proactive support

CALLS

Structured Clarity

Structured conversations that push the client, the team, and the workflow forward with clarity and purpose.

Primary Responsibility: Lead calls with high emotional intelligence and a strong question framework while allowing the client to speak most of the time.

CORE RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Prepare agendas for all calls
  • Ask high quality questions that uncover insights
  • Extract stories, frameworks, beliefs, and proof
  • Document insights and convert them into action items
  • Keep calls on time and on track
  • Conduct renewal and reset conversations

DELIVERABLE SYSTEMS

◉

CULTURE

The Team Engine

The standards, behaviors, and communication norms that shape how the Digital Leverage team functions together.

Primary Responsibility: Support the team by modeling clarity, ownership, and consistency in every interaction.

CORE RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Communicate clearly with writers, editors, producers, and leadership
  • Share best practices and help teammates improve
  • Support writers with clean briefs and clear direction
  • Protect team bandwidth by managing client demands
  • Uphold agency values in communication and delivery
  • Document processes and improve workflow efficiency

DELIVERABLE SYSTEMS

Writer Briefing Templates Cross-Team SOPs Feedback Guidelines Knowledge Base
◐

CAMPAIGNS

Content That Performs

All content outputs and strategic initiatives for each client, including threads, short form content, positioning, and story arcs.

Primary Responsibility: Turn client insights into structured content plans that deliver consistent performance.

CORE RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Research client industry and audience
  • Build content calendars and angles
  • Guide the writer with strategy, tone, and direction
  • Approve weekly threads and posts
  • Analyze performance data
  • Plan special campaigns around launches or events
